# Score distribution

The horizontal axis shows episode score; bar height counts episodes at that
score. Decide whether high scores are typical or rare. Use the supplied exact
episode counts, mean, and median. This chart does not show the order of episodes.

Use 1–2 sentences and at most 40 words. Do not infer improvement over time or
recommend settings from this chart.

Example with invented data: Of 500 episodes, 480 scored zero and 20 reached the
one-point high score, so success was rare.
