# Score trends

Compare the first 50 mean and last 50 mean from this same run.
State whether the final average is higher, lower,
or equal, and include both numbers. These compare the beginning and end;
they do not establish a steady trend throughout training.

The orange chart line is a trailing mean of up to 20 episodes, a different
window from the supplied 50-episode averages.

Use 1–2 sentences and at most 40 words. Describe this run and optionally suggest
one concrete value to try next. Read data_tool /run for current settings and
/run/rules for allowed values first. No theory or causal explanations. Ax3l
adds target comparisons later.

Example with invented data: The first 50 episodes averaged 1 point and the
final 50 averaged 2 points, so the final average was higher; try
model.hidden_size=240 next.
