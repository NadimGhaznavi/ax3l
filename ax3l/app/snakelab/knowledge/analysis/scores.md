# Score trends

Describe the direction of episode scores over time. The orange line is a
trailing mean of up to 20 episodes. An isolated high score does not establish
sustained improvement. The epsilon marker identifies when ordinary random
exploration ended; it does not establish why scores changed.

Use 1–2 sentences and at most 40 words. Describe this run, without recommending
settings or comparing against the target line; Ax3l adds target comparisons
later.

Example with invented data: Scores rose late in training, and the final 50
episodes averaged 2 points against a run-wide mean of 1 point.
