# Run the prompts as a scientific experiement

| When | Field | What Qwen records |
|---|---|---|
| Before | Research question | What am I trying to find out? |
| Before | Hypothesis and rationale | What do I predict, and why? |
| Before | Baseline | Which configuration or previous runs will I compare against? |
| Before | Planned changes | What will change, and what will stay fixed? |
| Before | Run plan | Configuration, epochs, seeds, repetitions, and why this budget is appropriate. |
| Before | Success criterion | Which metric will I measure, and what outcome would support the hypothesis? |
| After | Observed results | Actual measurements and links to runs and plots, including failures. |
| After | Assessment | Supported, contradicted, or inconclusive—with evidence and limitations. |
| After | Next step | Repeat, extend, revise, or explore another hypothesis—and why. |
